How to put it together
  1. Each page is labelled (A1, A2, B1…) with a little map showing where it goes. Row A is the top.
  2. No overlap: trim the margins off along the corner marks and butt the pages edge to edge, taping on the back.
  3. With overlap: trim the top and left margins of each page (except row A and column 1), then lay it over its neighbour so its edge sits on the dashed marks. Glue or tape.

Printing tips

  • In the share sheet choose Print, or your printer's own app (HP Smart, Epson, Canon…) if it's listed.
  • In the print options pick paper size A4 and load that paper.
  • If your printer app has a scaling option, choose 100% / actual size, not “fit to page”.
  • Sizes come out slightly small? Use Printer size check in Settings (⚙︎).

Tips

  • Install it: in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen. It then opens full screen and works offline.
  • Tap any photo in the preview to choose which part is kept when cropping.
  • Exact sizes are printed exactly. Turn on cut guides to make cutting out easier.
  • Your photos never leave your phone: everything is done on the device.
