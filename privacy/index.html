Privacy Policy

Print Sizing · Effective 10 October 2026

The short version

Print Sizing does not collect, store or share any personal data. Your photos never leave your device.

Your photos

You choose photos with the iPhone photo picker or from Files. Print Sizing only sees the photos you pick, and it only uses them to lay out and print your pages. All processing happens on your device. Photos are not uploaded, copied to a server or kept after you remove them from the app.

Printing and sharing

When you tap Print, your pages go to the printer you choose using Apple's AirPrint. When you tap Share PDF, the PDF goes only where you send it, such as Files or your printer's app. Those destinations are covered by their own privacy policies.

Settings

Your preferences, such as paper size and units, are saved on your device so the app remembers them. They are never sent anywhere.

No accounts, tracking or ads

There is no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code that collects data. The app makes no network connections.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Web version

The web version of Print Sizing at this site works the same way: everything happens in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub or see the support page.
